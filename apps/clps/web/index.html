What these results mean

Ranges are calculated lower and upper bounds, conditional on the terrain model and reported geometric directions. Wider ranges and UNKNOWN states remain visible; they do not prevent a result from being shown.

Earth-disk visibility describes the visible area of Earth. It is not Earth-center communications visibility or a radio link budget. Terrain height error is not bounded by this model. Six-hour samples do not establish exact event times.
